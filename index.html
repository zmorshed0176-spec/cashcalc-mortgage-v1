FAQ

Questions mortgage and protection advisers ask

Is FE CashCalc for mortgage and protection advisers?

Yes. Mortgage advisers get professional tools to model mortgage and protection scenarios. Holistic financial planners can connect a remortgage decision to a client's long-term plan without re‑keying across separate tools. Larger firms and networks get a single platform across both mortgage and financial advice teams.

Which calculators are included?

10 calculators and stress tests: mortgage overpayments, mortgage rate rise, reduced income, unemployment, life insurance, income protection, family income benefit, critical illness cover, combined life and critical illness cover, and a protection needs analysis.

Which mortgage types can I model?

Repayment, interest‑only and part‑and‑part mortgages, with fixed and variable rates, fees and overpayments, so each plan reflects the client's real arrangement.

How much does it cost?

£80/month + VAT per individual adviser licence, with mortgage and protection built in at no extra cost. A paraplanner licence is free with every individual adviser licence. For larger firms, contact us for a bespoke group price.

Where does Nexus for Financial Advisors fit in?

It's the FE fundinfo platform that FE CashCalc sits inside. For firms that also give financial advice, it links the client's plan to fund research in FE Analytics, so you can move between the two without switching systems.

I already use FE CashCalc. Do I need to set anything up?

No. Mortgage and protection is added to existing accounts automatically, with the two mortgage calculators and eight protection calculators included. There is no setup and no extra cost. For help and support, please contact your Account Manager.

What happens when I book a demo?

One of our specialists will arrange a short call to show you the mortgage and protection tools in FE CashCalc.
